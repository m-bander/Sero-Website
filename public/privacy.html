Privacy Policy for Sero Safe

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sero Safe ("we", "our", or "us"). Sero Safe is a privacy-first personal vault application designed to securely protect and encrypt your private photos, videos, and media files.

2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

We believe in minimal data collection and zero-knowledge privacy:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we access your basic profile information (such as your name and email address) to authenticate your account and identify your encrypted vault.
  • Google Drive App Data: With your explicit consent, Sero Safe requests access to the Google Drive Application Data folder (drive.appdata scope). This access is strictly used to store and restore your encrypted backup files. We do not access, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Encrypted Media: Your photos, videos, and local database are encrypted on your device using industry-standard AES-256 and RSA encryption before any backup occurs. We cannot read or decrypt your private content.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Sero Safe's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.

4. Data Retention and Security

All sensitive user keys and backups are protected by end-to-end encryption. You have full control over your data and can delete your account and all associated backups directly within the application settings at any time.
